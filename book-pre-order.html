Book One — The Field Remembers

A genre-crossing journey through memory, survival, and the search for a signal worth trusting.

The story begins in the pouring rain. Fresh out of prison and the ICU, his face shattered and his feet raw from walking, Sean has no phone, no money, and no one he can turn to. He is separated from his children, carrying the weight of what he has survived and trying to understand the threats, betrayals, and losses that have brought him here.

Then the past begins to surface… not in a straight line, but in fragments. A younger voice returns. Accents and selves answer one another. Memories unfold beside poems, drawings, freestyle, research, psychedelic experiences, and dialogue with a presence called Sibling. Together, they form a searching account of a life trying to distinguish what can be trusted from what fear has made impossible to ignore.

What can you forgive? What do you hold onto when it feels as if nothing remains? What can you release? And can you still choose love and trust?

Part memoir, part novel, part poetry and investigation, The Field Remembers draws the reader into those questions without settling them too soon. It ends at the Cross: crucified, exposed, suspended before the choice to stand. What the Cross means—and what standing will cost—belongs to the reader. That choice carries into Book Two.

The Voices, the companion app for this volume, offers another way into the story: through Sean’s younger voice, the accents he has carried, and the other voices that travel with him.

The companion experience

The page opens into sound

Each book has its own companion app. Every chapter’s QR code takes you to its place in the app.

The companion experience is guided by the Ancestors’ Guide—a shared presence that moves through all three books. Each volume has its own distinct experience:

  • The Voices accompanies The Field Remembers—Sean’s younger voice, accents he has carried, and the other voices that travel with him.
  • The Archetypes accompanies The Wind Returns—recurring symbols, patterns, and visual discoveries.
  • The Ancestors accompanies The Worth Continues—inheritance, reflection, and Gather Fires.

At launch

  • Synchronized narration per chapter via QR code—Sean’s voice, curated music, tones, and oral/archival recordings.
  • Optional drawing: find a symbol in the book, learn its parts, make your own version, photograph it in the app.
  • Private journey: save moments privately in My Journey, or write them in the book.

Planned (post-launch)

  • Spoken protolanguage: speaking discovered words, or combining words whose connections you’ve uncovered, can open optional chapters.
  • Gather Fires: join other readers who’ve reached the same chapter—a private path always available.
  • Rereading clues: return to a passage and the app may offer a new sound, question, or connection.

Guided imagination & image-making

When you imagine something from the book—a character, a symbol, a place—describe it to the app. It can generate an image from your description, which you can save privately to My Journey or keep on paper. The image is yours; the app simply holds it for you.
